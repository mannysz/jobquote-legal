Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 · Contact: app-support@monday.camp

1. Overview (Local-First Architecture)

JobQuote: Contractor Estimate ("JobQuote" or the "App") is built on a local-first architecture. We do not require account creation, and we do not operate a cloud database of your jobs, estimates, invoices, or client lists.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All business and job records you enter into the App are stored exclusively on your device, including:

  • Your business name, phone number, license info, trade selection, default tax rate, and custom Service List presets.
  • Your payment handle or payment link (e.g., Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, or Payment URL).
  • Your business logo image, job site photos, and on-screen customer signatures.
  • Client names, client phone numbers or job addresses, line items, pricing, and notes.

We do not collect, access, read, sell, or store any of this business or client data on our servers. When you export a PDF, CSV, or send an SMS/email, that data leaves your device solely through the native iOS share or messaging action you initiate.

3. Device Permissions

Camera & Photo Library: Requested only when you attach job-site photos to an estimate/invoice or upload a business logo. Selected photos remain stored locally on your device.

4. Analytics (PostHog)

To improve App reliability and understand feature usage, JobQuote uses PostHog to collect strictly non-PII, anonymized usage events (such as app opens, workflow steps, and paywall views). Client names, phone numbers/addresses, business names, license numbers, payment handles, signer names, and job dollar amounts are stripped on-device before any analytics event is sent.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Apple & RevenueCat)

JobQuote offers optional paid plans (Weekly Pro, Annual Pro, and Lifetime Pass) processed exclusively through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). We use RevenueCat to verify purchase receipts and manage your JobQuote Pro entitlement using an anonymous app user identifier and Apple transaction receipt data. We never receive or store your credit card number or Apple ID credentials.

6. Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

You may delete individual jobs, purge Recently Deleted items, or reset App data at any time inside the App. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored App data from your device (subject to your own iOS/iCloud device backup settings).

7. Children's Privacy

JobQuote is a business productivity tool intended for adult professionals and is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable).

8. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy by updating the Effective Date above. Questions? Email app-support@monday.camp.
