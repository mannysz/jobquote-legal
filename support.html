JobQuote Support & FAQ

Need help? Email app-support@monday.camp (1–2 business days response).

How do I restore my Pro subscription or Lifetime Pass?

Open JobQuote → tap Settings → tap Upgrade to Pro / View Plans → tap Restore Purchases using the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel my Weekly or Annual subscription?

Open iOS Settings → tap your Apple Account → tap Subscriptions → select JobQuote → tap Cancel Subscription.

Where is my data stored?

All estimates, invoices, photos, and signatures are stored locally on your iPhone so the app works offline. Keep iCloud Device Backup enabled and use Export CSV in Settings for bookkeeping.
